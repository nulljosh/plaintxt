# X thread: Plaintxt

1/ They don't make them like Sublime Text anymore. Editors are a div soup, a browser in a trench coat, or a subscription. Built Plaintxt to fix that: open a file, type, save. That's the whole app.

2/ It's a SwiftUI DocumentGroup around a TextEditor. Open, save, autosave, undo, find, dictation, VoiceOver, iCloud, all come from the OS, not from me. I added a monospaced toggle, a font size stepper, and a status line. Nothing else.

3/ Mac, iPhone and iPad from one target, plus a command line tool for counts. Free on every platform. plain.jaybulb.com
